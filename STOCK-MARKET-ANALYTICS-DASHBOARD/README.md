Stock Market Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Tableau dashboard** developed to analyse stock market performance, price trends, trading volume, turnover, volatility, and monthly performance. This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including **data cleaning and transformation using Microsoft Excel Power Query**, followed by data analysis and visualization in Tableau.

---

 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing historical stock market data to identify trends and patterns in stock prices, trading activity, turnover, and market volatility.

The raw stock market dataset was first **cleaned and transformed using Microsoft Excel Power Query** to improve data quality, consistency, and usability. The cleaned dataset was then imported into **Tableau** to develop an interactive dashboard containing KPIs, filters, trend analysis, and business-focused visualizations.

---

 Objectives

- Analyse historical stock price performance.
- Monitor total trading volume.
- Analyse total market turnover.
- Identify the highest closing price.
- Compare average closing prices across companies.
- Analyse stock volatility.
- Track trading volume trends over time.
- Analyse monthly stock performance.
- Provide interactive filters for detailed market analysis.
- Generate actionable insights from historical market data.

---

Data Cleaning & Transformation

 Microsoft Excel Power Query

A major part of this project was the **data cleaning and transformation performed using Microsoft Excel Power Query** before loading the data into Tableau.

 Data Preparation Tasks

- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing and null values.
- Corrected inconsistent data entries.
- Standardized stock symbols and categorical values.
- Converted date columns into appropriate date formats.
- Converted numerical columns into appropriate data types.
- Renamed columns for better readability.
- Checked data consistency and quality.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for Tableau analysis.

> **Key Highlight:** The raw dataset was not directly used for visualization. Data cleaning and transformation were performed in **Excel Power Query** before creating the Tableau dashboard.

Dashboard Features

 🔹 Closing Price Trend

A line chart showing the movement of closing prices across different years.

This helps identify:

- Price growth and decline
- Major market fluctuations
- Long-term price trends

---

 🔹 Turnover by Company

A treemap visualization comparing turnover generated by different companies.

This helps identify companies with significant trading activity and market turnover.

---

 🔹 Companies by Closing Price

Compares the average closing prices of selected companies to understand differences in stock price performance.

---

 🔹 Volatility Analysis

Analyses the average volatility of companies to identify stocks experiencing greater price fluctuations.

---

 🔹 Trading Volume Trend

An area chart showing changes in trading volume over time.

This helps identify periods of:

- High trading activity
- Low trading activity
- Significant market movements

---

 🔹 Monthly Performance

Analyses average closing prices across different months to identify monthly patterns and variations in stock performance.

---

 Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters for:

- **Year of Date**
- **Stock Symbol**
- **Month of Date**
- **Turnover Range**

These filters allow users to explore specific companies, time periods, and turnover ranges dynamically.
